0–3 months · Sleep
"Why won't she nap longer than 30 minutes?"

Short naps are normal at this age, and usually fix themselves around 4–5 months.

Newborns cycle through light sleep fast and often wake at the end of the first cycle. Aim for a calm, dark room and an awake window of 60–90 minutes.
