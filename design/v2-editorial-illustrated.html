Sleep · 0–3 months
Why won't she nap longer than 30 minutes?

That's normal at this age. Naps usually lengthen around 4–5 months.

Young babies cycle through light sleep quickly and often wake at the end of the first cycle. Keep the room dark, stay consistent, and keep awake windows to roughly 60–90 minutes.

3 of 4 books agree
"A 30–45 minute nap is a single sleep cycle, entirely typical in the early weeks."
